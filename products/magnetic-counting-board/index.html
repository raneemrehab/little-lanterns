Magnetic Rote Counting Board

A solid wood ten-frame with ten chunky numeral pieces. Each piece hides a magnet underneath and clicks into its matching recess, so children count, match and place 1 to 10 with their hands.

What it teaches

  • Rote counting 1 to 10Placing the pieces in order builds the counting sequence.
  • Number recognitionEach numeral is matched to its engraved twin.
  • Number formationRaised dots follow the writing path. Children start at the big dot and trace along with a finger.
  • Matching and visual discriminationTelling 6 from 9 and 1 from 7 by shape.
  • Problem solvingOnly the right numeral seats flat in each recess.
  • Fine motor skillsLifting, gripping, turning and placing each piece.
  • Hand-eye coordinationLining a piece up with its recess before it drops in.
  • Tactile feedbackBumpy tracing dots and a magnetic click tell the hands what the eyes see.

Suggested build: each square has a numeral-shaped well about 8 mm deep so the board also works as an inset puzzle. Solid beech board about 55 × 25 cm with 10 cm squares. Each numeral is about 8 cm tall and 2 cm thick, far too big to fit a small-parts test cylinder (3.2 cm wide), with 3 mm rounded edges with a water-based stain so the grain shows through.
